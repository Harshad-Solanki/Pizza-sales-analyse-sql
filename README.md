# 🍕 Pizza Sales Performance Analysis (SQL)

A relational database project focused on extracting actionable business insights from pizza store sales records using SQL. The analysis investigates sales volume, revenue drivers, hourly demand patterns, and product category rankings.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
The objective of this project is to analyze transactional sales data across multiple relational tables to optimize menu offerings, uncover revenue trends, and identify peak ordering hours for staffing and inventory planning[cite: 2].

### Key Questions Answered:
* What are the overall sales volume and total revenue generated[cite: 2]?
* Which pizza types and sizes drive the majority of orders[cite: 2]?
* What are the peak ordering times throughout the day[cite: 2]?
* What is the cumulative revenue trend over time[cite: 2]?
* Which products rank as the top 3 revenue generators within each category[cite: 2]?

---

## 🗄️ Database Schema
The project analyzes four relational tables[cite: 2]:
* **`orders`**: Contains `order_id`, `order_date`, and `order_time`[cite: 2].
* **`order_details`**: Captures `order_details_id`, `order_id`, `pizza_id`, and `quantity`[cite: 2].
* **`pizzas`**: Stores `pizza_id`, `pizza_type`, `size`, and `price`[cite: 2].
* **`pizza_types`**: Details `pizza_type_id`, `name`, and `category`[cite: 2].

---

## 🛠️ SQL Techniques & Concepts Used
* **Multi-Table Joins:** Connected transactional tables (`orders`, `order_details`) with product catalogs (`pizzas`, `pizza_types`) via `INNER JOIN`[cite: 2].
* **Aggregations & Grouping:** Calculated KPIs using `COUNT()`, `SUM()`, `AVG()`, `ROUND()`, along with `GROUP BY` and `ORDER BY`[cite: 2].
* **Datetime Functions:** Extracted hourly distribution using `EXTRACT(HOUR FROM order_time)` to identify peak demand spikes[cite: 2].
* **Subqueries & Derived Tables:** Formulated nested queries to calculate daily sales averages and category percentage contributions[cite: 2].
* **Advanced Window Functions:**
  * Running totals: `SUM(revenue) OVER(ORDER BY order_date)` for cumulative revenue analysis[cite: 2].
  * Partitioned ranking: `RANK() OVER(PARTITION BY category ORDER BY revenue DESC)` to isolate top-performing products per category[cite: 2].

---

## 📊 Key Insights
* **Peak Ordering Hours:** Order volumes peak during specific lunch and dinner time slots, highlighting optimal kitchen staffing windows[cite: 2].
* **Category Contribution:** Visualized the percentage of total sales generated by distinct pizza categories to evaluate menu profitability[cite: 2].
* **Top Performers:** Identified top-selling SKUs by quantity and revenue to prevent stockouts of high-demand ingredients[cite: 2].
